The uncertainty surrounding both the field and many of the problems it addresses made the journey challenging, but reaching the end of this work makes the entire process worthwhile.

Finally, I would like to thank my family, friends, and everyone close to me for their patience, encouragement, and support throughout this process. Their presence during both productive and difficult periods made this journey considerably easier.

To everyone who contributed to this journey, directly or indirectly, my sincere gratitude.
